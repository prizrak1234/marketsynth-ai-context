# Commercial MVP P0.5 — Review and approval boundary

## Domain actions

submit-review · approve · reject · return-draft · supersede · archive

## Records

actor, timestamp, note, previous/new status, evidence snapshot hash (in metadata `review_events`).

## Approve means

Confirms commercial viability decision for that Evidence snapshot.

## Approve does **not** mean

- Strategy created
- MarketingPlan approved
- ExecutionApproval created
- PublicationApproval created
- Budget approval

Verdict approval ≠ Execution approval ≠ Publication approval.
