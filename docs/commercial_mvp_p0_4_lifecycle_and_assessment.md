# Commercial MVP P0.4 — Lifecycle and assessment

## Lifecycle

`draft` → `under_review` → `accepted` | `rejected` · supersede / archive

Accepted Evidence is immutable (claim/links). Assessment may be marked conflicting/outdated without becoming Verdict.

## Assessment

`unverified` | `confirmed` | `partial` | `conflicting` | `missing` | `outdated`

Example: lifecycle=`under_review`, assessment=`conflicting`.
