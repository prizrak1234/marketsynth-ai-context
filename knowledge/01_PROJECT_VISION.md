## Mission

Help an entrepreneur pass the full path from **idea to scaling** — with evidence-backed decisions before spend, and professional execution after approval.

Near-term commercial wedge: **validate before spend, then launch with evidence** (BIV → Strategy → Readiness → Campaign → Execution).

---

## Vision

**Marketsynth** is an **AI Business Operating System** — a platform to create, launch, and grow a business. Marketing is one subsystem, not the whole product.

Positioning (2026-07): *Marketsynth — AI-платформа для создания, запуска и развития бизнеса.*

---

## Core philosophy

1. **Business-first, not tool-first** — understand goal, audience, offer, metric before invoking tools.
2. **Evidence before verdict** — no decorative confidence; findings must be substantiated.
3. **Explicit execution** — user confirms critical steps; no silent auto-run of skills, publishing, or paid actions.
4. **Honest capability** — show gaps when stages are skipped or data is insufficient.
5. **One operating loop** — observe → decide with evidence → approve → execute → measure → learn.

---

## Commercial principles

From [commercial-product-directive](../.cursor/rules/commercial-product-directive.mdc):

- Every slice must shorten path to first paying customer or increase value of a sellable workflow.
- **CWF.1 Launch Pack boundary:** validation, verdict, risks, audience, positioning, offer, launch plan, 3 Telegram posts, 1–3 visuals, one real Telegram publish, persisted project history.
- Forbidden in product UI: JSON as UI, fake progress, proceed without evidence, publish without human approval, dry-run presented as real publish.
- Priority classes A (revenue), B (reliability), C (commercial safety) only — defer D/E.

Strategic invariant (Platform Map):

> **Сначала проверить и сохранить деньги клиента — потом помогать инвестировать в развитие.**

## Target users

| Segment | Need |
|---------|------|
| **Solo entrepreneur** | Validate idea, get launch pack, publish first content without hiring agency |
| **Small business owner** | Run campaigns with explicit next actions, not agent wiring |
| **Marketsynth owner (pilot)** | Commercial golden path demonstrable in browser with real external calls |

Users describe **business goals** ("dental leads", "launch SaaS"), not infrastructure ("run Wordstat node 3").

## Competitive advantage

- **Agent OS** with governed Skills, Tools, Workflows, Supervisor — not a chatbot or agent builder.
- **Evidence-backed BIV** with commercial verdict (GO / CONDITIONAL / PILOT / HOLD / NO_GO).
- **Subsystem Standard** — lifecycle, manifest, operator, honest readiness for substantial capabilities.
- **Knowledge Governance** — citations, lineage, no VectorDB hallucination layer in v1.
- **Integrated conveyor** — Research → Campaign → Content → Media → Publishing (Telegram) with approval gates.

## Project scope

### In scope (active commercial track)

- CWF.1: Idea → Research → Evidence → Verdict → Offer → Launch Plan → Content → Telegram
- CMVP.1 Business Idea Validator (accepted; integrity repair in PRODUCT-01.3)
- Marketing department v2 (14 roles, frozen)
- Business Campaign Layer + Control/Action Center + Operator
- Publishing to Telegram (gated real send)
- Video Studio i2v path (frozen except P0 fixes)

### Explicitly out of scope (unless new phase approved)

- Skill/MCP marketplace
- CRM, Ads billing, analytics dashboard as product
- Multi-channel publish (Instagram/LinkedIn)
- LangGraph marketing orchestration
- Background workers for campaign/skill auto-execution
- VS.2B+ video expansion while VIDEO track frozen
- Digital Identity System implementation until CGP.10C + vertical slices accepted
